# Week 15 persona test

**David Buzali · Synthetic simulation · 19 November 2026**

## Research basis and task

Adriana is a fictional RR. HH. lead in a Mexican textile plant. She has five minutes before setting the screening criteria for a second-shift quality-control vacancy. OCC's employer guide places experience, schooling, skills and shift before publication. That supports the choice of moment and task. Adriana's identity, time pressure and work situation are invented.

- [OCC employer publication guide](https://supportcenter.occ.com.mx/hc/es/articles/41245639854737-Publica-tu-primera-vacante-en-OCC)

**Test prompt used in the existing build conversation at David's direction.** As Adriana, walk through the home page, case, four-select rehearsal and generated agreement. Say what you would select and what you would do for a real vacancy. Flag one concrete point of confusion. Treat these as synthetic tasks and report browser checks separately.

## Walkthrough and response

| Screen | Observed in the simulation |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Adriana opened “Ver caso de turno” because she was defining initial screens. |
| Case | She understood that RR. HH. routes a permitted work description and Calidad asks for a criterion-linked clarification. |
| Rehearsal | She chose the defect/action/check criterion, a written description, the quality lead and two working days. |
| Result | The result was a useful draft. She could not tell who would accept the case in the live process or when she should involve them. |

> “The agreement gave me a useful draft, but I still needed to know who accepts the case in our existing process and when to involve them.”

## Change and retest

The revised result and downloaded text now say to confirm who receives a case, what makes it eligible, and the review date with RR. HH. and Calidad before publication, using the company's internal channel. The rehearsal makes clear that the agreement is created in the browser. The simulated user can now identify the next step: agree with Calidad on an eligible case, a reviewer and a calendar date before publishing, then use the existing internal process.

This was a same-conversation simulation requested by David. The exercise supports a wording change. It provides no real audience response, employer adoption or savings measurement.
